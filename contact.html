How to Find Us

🚗 By Car

Exit the M4 at Junction 11 and follow the A33 towards Reading town centre. Turn left onto Caversham Road. Free parking on-site.

🚌 By Bus

Buses 17, 23, and 31 stop on Caversham Road within a 3-minute walk. Reading Buses Journey Planner: readingbuses.co.uk

🚂 By Train

Reading Railway Station is 12 minutes on foot or 5 minutes by taxi. Trains from London Paddington every 10 minutes.

🚲 By Bike

Secure covered bike storage is available to all members. We're on the Sustrans National Cycle Network Route 422.
