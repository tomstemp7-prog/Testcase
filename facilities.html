Iron & Oak

Built to a Different Standard

When we designed Iron & Oak, we asked a simple question: what would the ideal club look like if you started from scratch? The answer is this. Every sq ft has been thought through, from the layout of the gym floor to the temperature of the plunge pool. This is what premium looks like.

01

Gym Floor

Our 8,000 sq ft main gym floor is equipped exclusively with Life Fitness Hammer Strength and Technogym kit — the same brands used by elite sports clubs and performance centres. The layout is designed to flow naturally between cardio, resistance, and functional zones.

A dedicated free-weights area features dumbbells up to 60kg, an Olympic lifting platform, power racks, and cable machines. The functional training zone offers battle ropes, plyometric boxes, TRX stations, and sleds.

  • Life Fitness & Technogym cardio machines (treadmills, bikes, ellipticals, rowers)
  • Olympic lifting platform with Eleiko barbells
  • Hammer Strength plate-loaded equipment
  • Dedicated stretching and mobility zone
  • Complimentary towel service
  • On-floor coaching available weekday mornings
View Membership Options
02

Swimming Pool

Our 25-metre, 6-lane competition pool is maintained at 28°C year-round. Dedicated lane swimming is available throughout the day alongside structured swim sessions, aqua fitness classes, and junior lessons. The pool deck is furnished with premium recliners for post-swim relaxation.

A separate learner pool (10m × 8m, maintained at 32°C) hosts all junior swimming lessons, mother-and-baby sessions, and low-impact aqua therapy classes.

  • 25m × 6-lane heated competition pool (28°C)
  • Separate learner and aqua fitness pool (32°C)
  • Poolside towel and refreshment service
  • Weekly structured swim sessions and coaching
  • Junior swimming lessons (all ASA levels)
  • Aqua HIIT and aqua yoga classes
Club & Premium Access
03

Spa & Sauna

The Iron & Oak spa is a sanctuary of recovery and restoration — a thermal suite designed to complement your training, not just reward it. The combination of heat, cold, and steam therapy has been proven to accelerate muscle recovery, improve sleep quality, and reduce cortisol.

Our spa team offers a curated menu of treatments including sports massage, deep tissue massage, facials, and hydrotherapy sessions — bookable separately or as part of a spa day package.

  • Traditional Finnish sauna (85–95°C)
  • Aromatherapy steam room (44°C)
  • Ice plunge pool (8°C)
  • Heated relaxation loungers
  • Infrared cabin (available for private hire)
  • Full treatment menu — sports massage, facials, hydrotherapy
Premium Spa Access
04

Group Studios

Four dedicated group fitness spaces — each designed and equipped for a specific style of training. Studio 1 handles our high-energy HIIT and functional classes. Studio 2 is dedicated to mind-body work: yoga, pilates, and breathwork. The Cycle Studio houses 30 Schwinn AC Performance bikes with immersive lighting and audio. The Boxing Suite is kitted with Everlast bags, mitts, and floor-to-ceiling mirrors.

  • 4 dedicated group fitness studios (550–800 sq ft each)
  • Dedicated cycle studio — 30 Schwinn AC Performance bikes
  • Yoga & pilates studio — sprung hardwood floor, full mirror wall
  • Boxing suite — Everlast equipment, bags, and mitts
  • Premium AV systems and pro-grade sound in all studios
  • 60+ instructor-led classes per week
View Full Timetable
05

Tennis & Padel Courts

Iron & Oak boasts three floodlit outdoor hard courts and two state-of-the-art glass-backed indoor padel courts — one of Reading's only padel facilities. Bookable through the app by Premium members, with guest booking available at an additional charge.

Our resident tennis coach offers individual and group lessons across all levels, from complete beginners through to LTA-rated league players. Club leagues and social round-robins run throughout the year.

  • 3 outdoor floodlit hard courts (Deco Turf surface)
  • 2 indoor glass-backed padel courts
  • Court booking via app (Premium members)
  • LTA-affiliated coaching — individual and group
  • Junior tennis academy (ages 5–16)
  • Racket hire available (Wilson Pro Staff)
Premium Court Access
06

Business Lounge

Work from the club. The Iron & Oak Business Lounge is a quiet, well-designed workspace available exclusively to Premium members — ideal for remote workers who want to break up the day with a workout, a swim, or a sauna session without losing productive hours.

High-speed Wi-Fi, charging stations, private booths, a boardroom table (bookable for meetings), and direct café table service make this a genuinely premium working environment — not just a corner with a Wi-Fi password.

  • Dedicated quiet workspace for Premium members
  • High-speed fibre broadband (dedicated line)
  • Private meeting booths and 8-person boardroom
  • Café table service — breakfast, lunch, and barista coffee
  • Printing and scanning facilities
  • Open 6am–9pm weekdays
