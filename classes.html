Signature Classes

Our Most Loved Sessions

Every class is designed by our in-house experts to deliver measurable results — and be genuinely enjoyable.

💥

Oak HIIT

Our flagship high-intensity interval session — 45 minutes of structured work/rest intervals combining plyometrics, kettlebells, and resistance training. Built for maximum calorie burn and metabolic conditioning. Suitable for all fitness levels with scalable movements.

⏱ 45 min🔥 High intensity👤 All levels📍 Studio 1
🚴

Cycle Power

A music-driven indoor cycling class that mixes endurance climbs, sprints, and rhythm rides. Led by our Schwinn-certified instructors, this 50-minute session builds cardiovascular fitness and lower-body strength simultaneously. Expect sweat. Expect results.

⏱ 50 min🔥 High intensity👤 All levels📍 Cycle Studio
🧘

Vinyasa Flow

A dynamic and meditative yoga class linking breath to movement. Each 60-minute session follows a thoughtful sequence building strength, flexibility, and body awareness. Suitable for those with some yoga experience. Props provided. Small class sizes for personal attention.

⏱ 60 min💚 Low–Med intensity👤 Intermediate📍 Yoga Studio
🥊

Iron Boxing

Non-contact boxing fitness combining bag work, pad drills, and boxing-inspired conditioning circuits. Our most adrenaline-fuelled class — perfect for stress relief and functional fitness. All experience levels welcome; gloves and pads provided in the studio.

⏱ 45 min🔥 High intensity👤 All levels📍 Boxing Suite
🌙

Yin Yoga

A deeply restorative practice targeting the connective tissue — held poses of 3–5 minutes create profound flexibility gains. Ideal as an evening class to decompress, or as active recovery after intense training days. Bolsters, blocks, and blankets provided.

⏱ 60 min💚 Low intensity👤 All levels📍 Yoga Studio
🏋️

Pilates Core

Joseph Pilates' classical method brought to life by our specialist instructors — mat-based movements focusing on core stability, postural alignment, and mind-body connection. Particularly popular with runners, cyclists, and desk-bound professionals.

⏱ 55 min💛 Med intensity👤 All levels📍 Studio 2
