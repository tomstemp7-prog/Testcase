Membership & Contracts

No. All Iron & Oak memberships are rolling monthly — there is no minimum contract period. You can cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice (via the member app or by emailing us), and you'll never be charged a cancellation fee.

If you choose our Annual plan to save the equivalent of two months' fees, the annual amount is charged upfront. In this case, if you cancel early, we refund the remaining months proportionally (less any introductory discount applied at sign-up).

No joining fee, ever. We think joining fees are a relic of an older model — a way to monetise the moment you're most enthusiastic. We'd rather earn your loyalty over time. All you pay is your first month's membership from the date you join.

We also offer a free first week for all new members so you can experience the club before your first payment is taken. Simply book a tour, come in, and your trial starts that day.

Yes. Members can freeze their membership for up to 3 months per calendar year, in blocks of 1 month. Common reasons include holidays, injury, illness, or work commitments. To freeze, simply request it via the member app or contact our membership team at least 5 days before your next billing date.

During a freeze, no payment is taken and your access is temporarily suspended. When you unfreeze, your account resumes exactly as it was — no re-joining, no admin fee.

Absolutely. You can change your membership tier at any time through the member app or by speaking to our front-desk team. Upgrades take effect immediately (with a prorated charge for the remaining days of the current month). Downgrades take effect from the start of the next billing cycle.

Yes to both. For families, you can add children under 16 to any Club or Premium plan for £12 per child per month — this gives them access to junior swim sessions, junior classes, and supervised gym inductions (16+ only for unsupervised gym use). Partners can be added at 15% off the standard monthly rate.

For businesses, we offer corporate membership packages starting from 5 employees. Corporate members benefit from discounted rates, flexible billing, and a dedicated account manager. Contact our corporate team at corporate@ironoakclub.co.uk for a tailored quote.

If you relocate more than 15 miles from the club, or if a medical professional advises you that you are unable to use the club's facilities, you may cancel your membership immediately with no penalty, regardless of any annual commitment. We'll need to see supporting documentation (a letter from your GP, or proof of change of address), and we'll process the cancellation and any applicable refund within 5 working days.

Facilities

Iron & Oak is open Monday to Friday 6:00am–10:00pm, Saturday 7:00am–8:00pm, and Sunday 8:00am–6:00pm. Bank holidays follow weekend hours unless otherwise advertised.

The spa and pool have slightly reduced closing times (9:30pm weekdays) to allow for maintenance and preparation. The business lounge is open Monday to Friday 6:00am–9:00pm only.

Yes — free parking is included with all membership tiers. We have 180 spaces in our private car park directly adjacent to the club. The car park is gated and ANPR-controlled; your vehicle registration is linked to your membership account, so the barrier lifts automatically on arrival. EV charging is available on 12 bays (first-come, first-served; payable at the standard public rate).

No — complimentary fresh towels are available throughout the club for all members. Simply take what you need from the dispensers in the changing rooms, pool area, and spa. Used towels are collected and replaced continuously throughout the day. We do ask members not to remove towels from the premises, and not to use gym floor towels in the pool or spa area (separate towels are provided for each zone).

The gym floor, pool, spa, and studios are all mixed-gender. We have fully separate male and female changing rooms and shower facilities. We also have private changing cubicles available in all changing areas for those who prefer additional privacy. The spa relaxation lounge and pool deck are mixed-gender with a smart-casual code (robes provided in the spa changing area).

Lane booking is available to Premium members and can be done up to 5 days in advance through the member app. Club and Essential members have access to open lane swimming on a first-come basis. During peak hours (7–9am and 5–7pm on weekdays), we operate a managed lane system with slow, medium, and fast lanes clearly marked. Our lifeguard team are always on hand to help with lane etiquette.

Yes — the Oak Kitchen café is open daily from 7:00am until 30 minutes before closing. The menu focuses on fresh, nutritionist-designed food: post-workout shakes and smoothies, grain bowls, salads, wraps, and a full hot breakfast menu. We also serve artisan barista coffee throughout the day. Members receive a 5–15% discount (depending on tier) on all café purchases.

Classes

All classes are bookable through the Iron & Oak member app (iOS and Android) or through the member portal at members.ironoakclub.co.uk. Standard booking opens 7 days before a class. Premium members can book 48 hours ahead of this window, meaning they get first access to popular sessions.

Classes can also be booked in person at the front desk. If a class is fully booked, you can join the waitlist — spaces frequently open up within 24–48 hours before a session as members cancel or reschedule.

We ask for at least 2 hours' notice if you can no longer attend a booked class, to allow waitlisted members to take your place. Cancelling with less than 2 hours' notice, or failing to show up, will result in a late-cancel strike on your account. Three late-cancel strikes in a calendar month will result in a 48-hour booking restriction.

We understand that life happens — if you have exceptional circumstances, just drop us a message via the app and we'll review it.

Yes — the vast majority of our classes are designed to be accessible to all fitness levels, including complete beginners. Our instructors always offer modifications for every movement, and we encourage members to work at their own pace. Classes such as Oak HIIT, Pilates Core, and Vinyasa Flow explicitly welcome all levels.

That said, some classes are marked as Intermediate or Advanced in the app — we'd recommend building a few weeks of base fitness before attempting these. If you're ever unsure, just ask an instructor before the session begins.

Club members can bring 2 guests per month and Premium members can bring unlimited guests. Guests pay a day pass fee of £22, which includes full club access including classes. Guests must be accompanied by the member and pre-registered 24 hours in advance via the app or at reception. Under-18 guests must be accompanied at all times.

In most cases, nothing beyond appropriate sports clothing and clean trainers. All class-specific equipment (mats, blocks, gloves, kettlebells, cycle shoes) is provided within each studio. You're welcome to bring your own mat if you prefer. We ask members to use clean trainers in all studios — outdoor footwear should be changed before entering.

Getting Started

Welcome — we're glad you're here. When you join, you'll be invited to a complimentary 45-minute induction with one of our coaches. They'll show you around the gym floor, explain how the equipment works, talk you through any health or fitness goals you have, and help you put together a simple starting programme. No intimidation, no jargon — just a proper introduction to your new club.

After your induction, you'll have access to our new-member digital toolkit via the app — a 4-week starter programme, a class guide, and a nutrition resource written by our in-house nutritionist.

Full membership is available from age 16. Members aged 16–17 can use the gym floor and attend group classes but must complete a supervised induction and are not permitted to use free weights above 20kg without a PT present.

Children under 16 can access the club through our junior add-on (available with Club and Premium adult memberships) for swimming and junior class participation. All junior members under 14 must be accompanied by an adult member at all times.

Yes — we offer a complimentary club tour that includes a guided walkthrough of all facilities with a member of our team. If you'd like to actually train before joining, new members receive a free first week with no commitment required. Just book a tour through our website or by calling us, and we'll set you up on a trial from your first visit.

Yes — all new members complete a short Par-Q (Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire) during the sign-up process. This is standard practice across all reputable fitness facilities and is designed to flag any health considerations our team should be aware of. If you disclose any relevant health conditions, we may ask for GP clearance before you begin training — this is for your safety and is processed quickly in most cases.

The Iron & Oak member app is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play) — search for "Iron & Oak Fitness". Once you've joined, you'll receive an email with your login credentials and a direct download link. The app lets you book classes, manage your membership, check the timetable, access your digital membership card, track your workouts, and message our coaching team directly.

Payments & Billing

Monthly memberships are billed by Direct Debit on the same date each month as your original join date. For example, if you joined on the 14th, your Direct Debit processes on the 14th of each month. Annual memberships are charged as a single payment at sign-up.

You'll receive an email receipt for every payment. If a Direct Debit fails for any reason, we'll notify you immediately and attempt to re-collect within 7 days. Your access will not be suspended during this window — we'd always rather speak to you first.

Monthly memberships are collected by Direct Debit only (UK bank accounts). Annual memberships can be paid by Direct Debit, debit card, or credit card. Day passes, spa treatments, PT sessions, and café purchases can be paid by contactless card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay at reception or via the app.

Yes — our annual billing option saves you the equivalent of 2 months' membership fees. The annual prices are: Essential £348/year (saving £72), Club £552/year (saving £108), Premium £756/year (saving £144). The full amount is charged upfront. If you cancel an annual plan early, unused complete months are refunded proportionally.

We review our pricing annually, typically in January. If we need to increase prices, we give all existing members a minimum of 30 days' written notice before the new price applies. You're always free to cancel during this notice period without penalty if you don't wish to continue at the new price. We aim to keep any increases in line with inflation and commensurate with ongoing investment in the club.

Included PT sessions (part of Club or Premium membership) roll over by one month if unused — so if you don't use your PT session in March, you'll have two available in April. After that, unused inclusive sessions expire. Additional PT sessions purchased separately are valid for 3 months from purchase and are refundable within 14 days of purchase if unused. Class credits (for Essential members) reset each billing cycle and do not roll over.

Yes. We offer a 15% discount on all membership tiers for NHS staff, active military and veterans, emergency service workers, and full-time students (with valid student ID). Please bring proof of eligibility when you join or when upgrading, and the discount will be applied immediately. These discounts cannot be combined with promotional offers.
