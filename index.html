The Iron & Oak Difference

Built for Those Who Demand More

We set the standard for premium fitness in Berkshire — combining cutting-edge facilities with a warm, inclusive community.

World-Class Facilities

Over 22,000 sq ft of premium space — from our flagship gym floor with the latest Life Fitness equipment to a 25-metre competition pool.

Expert Personal Trainers

A team of over 20 fully qualified coaches — from strength and conditioning specialists to yoga masters and swimming coaches.

Spa & Recovery

Restore and recharge in our thermal spa suite — featuring a Finnish sauna, steam room, ice plunge pool, and relaxation lounge.
